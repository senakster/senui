Add `Button`: a native `<button>` (`type="button"` by default) with `primary`, `secondary`, `ghost` and `danger` variants in three sizes. `loading` keeps focus and width and ignores activation; icon-only buttons take an `icon` and require `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. Styles: `@senakster/react/styles.css` or `@senakster/react/styles/Button.css`.

Add the `--senui-motion-easing-linear` token, for continuous motion such as the loading spinner.
